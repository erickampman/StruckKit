### Added

- Physically modelled drum kit in one instance: snare with wires, two-headed toms, crashes, ride and
  hi-hats, plus an octave of hand drums and percussion.
- Controls in physical quantities: cymbal size and thickness, snare wire tension, tom tuning.
- Its own key map, a whole kit on one track.
- CLAP plugin for Linux (x86-64) and macOS 26 or later (Apple Silicon).
